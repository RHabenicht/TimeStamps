TimeStamps 2.5 startup splash

- The splash appears when TimeStamps starts, not while installing or uninstalling.
- A topmost and foreground popup presents the approved dice-free NightLife serpent image.
- An embedded NightLife serpent logo icon is explicitly rendered on the right side and reported in splash_status.txt. WM_PAINT is explicitly requested and graphics errors have a readable text fallback.
- The main window is created after the six-second splash period.
- Last status: %APPDATA%\TimeStamps\splash_status.txt.
- Failure details: %APPDATA%\TimeStamps\splash_errors.log.
- Verify actual rendered pixels; the existence of a window is not proof artwork appeared.
- Hosted Windows testing does not establish visibility on another physical computer.
